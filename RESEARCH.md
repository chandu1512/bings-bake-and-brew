## Heritage (from bingsbakery.com)
- 1871: founded as Fader's Bakery. Gotleib Fader delivered by horse-drawn wagon.
- 1946: Russell & Selena Bing bought it and renamed it Bing's Bakery.
- 2005: head baker Tom Guzzi and his wife Carla bought it.
- Billed as Delaware's longest-running bakery. Mashed ranked it the #1 bakery in Delaware, Eat This named its chocolate cake the best in the state, and the Cake Boss visited in 2014. Known for its carrot cake.
- Bake & Brew started in 2019–2020. The pastries come from Bing's Bakery at 253 E Main St.

## What reviewers say
Praise: friendly staff, fast service, a welcoming atmosphere with good music, the baked goods (crumb cake, donuts), and espresso drinks.
Complaints: drinks served lukewarm, lattes with too much milk, bitter cold brew, some pastries dry. The old site also caused problems: prices missing, no photos, an empty "Savory" section, no menu split by store, and a 2023 footer.
What the new site needs to do: give each store its own menu with all prices, show real photos, link straight to maps, make ordering and gift cards easy to reach, and show live open/closed status.
